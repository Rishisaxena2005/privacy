Privacy Policy – JoJo Booking Assistant

Last updated: October 21, 2026

JoJo Booking Assistant ("we", "our") provides a booking service that customers can use by sending messages to our WhatsApp Business number. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you message us on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name, and the content of the messages you send, including any booking details you provide (such as service, date, and time).

How we use your information

We use this information only to respond to your messages, create and manage your bookings, and send you booking-related updates. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Sharing and storage

Messages are delivered to us through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and stored in a secured database hosted by Supabase. We share information only with these service providers, and only as needed to operate the service, or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide the service. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing us at the address below. We will delete it within 30 days.
